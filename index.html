Why I Built This

I originally built this system for myself because I wanted complete control over how I tracked money and understood my spending behaviour. Over time, something unexpected happened. The simple act of manually logging expenses through Telegram started changing how I spent money in daily life. I became more aware of emotional purchases, unnecessary spending, and recurring habits that previously went unnoticed.
